Nhím

A Vietnamese learning app for people whose family already speaks it.

Why I built it

I'm Audrey Pham, a Communication Sciences and Disorders student at California State University, Fullerton. I study speech-language pathology, and I care about bilingual speakers.

Many Vietnamese American students understand Vietnamese at home but can't read it, write it, or say it back with confidence. Most apps aren't built for them. They teach one accent, treat tones as a footnote, and never mention that "I" and "you" change depending on who you're talking to.

Nhím (Vietnamese for "hedgehog") is built for them first.

Tones come first

Vietnamese has six tones. Change the pitch and you change the word:

maghost
mámother
màbut
mảgrave
mãhorse
mạrice seedling

Nhím draws every tone as a pitch line. With the tone mirror, you say a word into your microphone and see your own pitch line on top of the target. It scores the shape of the line, so a high or low voice is treated the same.

Built around family

Family voicesA parent or grandparent records words, and Nhím plays their voice instead of a computer voice.
Talk to familySix conversations, with Bà, Ông, Ba, a cousin, a friend's mom, and a market seller. Your pronouns and politeness change how they react.
Family chatRead texts the way families send them, without accent marks ("con an com chua"), and reply.
North and SouthBoth are always accepted. In Southern mode, hỏi and ngã count as the same tone, because they sound the same.
Type VietnameseA built-in Telex trainer, because many heritage speakers have never typed the language.
16 units, 16 storiesFrom greetings and family words to Tết, with a tap-through family tree for pronouns.

What's next: a study

I plan to run a small study with Vietnamese American heritage speakers: a tone listening test before and after four weeks of using Nhím, to see whether seeing pitch helps people hear and say the tones.

Native speaker review of the Vietnamese content is always welcome. You can open an issue on GitHub.

Private by design

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
  • Progress and family recordings stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • The tone mirror listens only while you tap the mic. Nothing is recorded or saved.
